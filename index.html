Makes the glasses buzz at full strength every 1.5 seconds so you can hear them. Stops when you pick them up, tap Stop, or after 1 minute. They need to be connected (within about 10 m).

A short paced-breathing exercise to reset when you feel tense or scattered. The glasses guide each breath, so you can close your eyes: a buzz that swells means breathe in, a soft bump means hold, a buzz that fades means breathe out.

Break reminders

Remind me to get up
During a session, the glasses buzz 2 long pulses when you have not got up for this long, then every 10 minutes until you walk around for a bit. Turning your head while sitting does not count as moving.

Auto-pause

Pause when I take them off
When the glasses sit completely still (on a desk, folded, charging), the session pauses: no alerts, timers stop, and that time is left out of your stats. Put them back on and they resume with one soft bump. If it pauses while you are wearing them, choose a longer time.

Taps and nods

Nod or shake to answer a buzz
  • Double-tap: tap the outside of the arm twice, quickly. With Snooze, double-tap again to cancel the snooze.
  • After a head-down alert: nod = snooze 10 minutes, shake = restart the timer.
  • After a break reminder: nod = taking the break now, shake = remind me in 20 minutes.
  • Alarm or Find buzzing: nod or shake stops it. Double-tap also stops it unless double-tap is set to Nothing.
  • Nods and shakes are 3 quick swings and only count for 15 seconds after a buzz.
